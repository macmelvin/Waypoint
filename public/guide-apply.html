Common questions

Is there a cost to apply?

No -- applying and getting listed is free. You only pay the 10% revenue share on tours actually booked through Waypoint.

Do I need an STB license?

Yes -- Waypoint only lists licensed guides, so tourists can book with confidence.

How and when do I pay the revenue share?

Visitors pay you directly, however you normally arrange it. Waypoint invoices you separately, biweekly, for the 10% revenue share -- payable by scanning the QR code in the application below.

Can I pick which neighbourhoods I'm listed for?

Yes -- select every neighbourhood or landmark you guide in the application below, and you can always ask Melvin to update it later.
