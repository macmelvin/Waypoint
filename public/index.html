🏟️ National Stadium (Kallang)
17–18 Oct 2026
25 Nov 2026
17 / 19 / 20 / 22 Dec 2026

MRT: Stadium (CC6, Circle Line) has direct access. Gates 1–5 and 18–23: Exit A, turn right, up the escalator to the L3 Promenade. Gates 6–17: Exit B, turn left, same escalator.

Walkable stations: Tanjong Rhu (TE23), about 12 min / 750 m. Mountbatten (CC7) or Kallang (EW10), about 15 min / 900 m each.

Bus: 10, 14, 14A, 16, 16M, 70, 70A, 70M, 196 at stop 80219 "National Stadium". Bus 11 at Stadium Stn (80199), closer to Gates 1–7 and 20–23.

Ride-hail / taxi: Kallang Wave Mall pick-up (Gates 1–5, 13–23) or Stadium Boulevard pick-up bay (Gates 5–13).

After the show: Mountbatten or Nicoll Highway MRT can be quicker than Stadium itself once crowds build up.

Sources: The Kallang · MustShareNews

🎸 Singapore Indoor Stadium
13 Oct 2026
18 Oct 2026
4 Nov 2026
10–11 Nov 2026

MRT: Stadium (CC6, Circle Line), Exit A — walk straight into OCBC Square, then turn left to the Indoor Stadium.

Walkable stations: Tanjong Rhu (TE23) Exit 2, about 5 min / 350 m. Mountbatten (CC7) Exit A, about 20 min / 1.3 km.

Bus: Service 11 stops right outside (Stadium Stn 80199, or opposite the venue at 80191), about 3 min walk. Services 10, 14, 14A, 16, 16M, 70, 70A, 70M, 196 stop near National Stadium, about 14–15 min walk.

Ride-hail: Pick-up Bay B1. Taxi: Stand I28.

Source: The Kallang

🎭 The Star Theatre (Buona Vista)
2 Nov 2026
16 Nov & 3 Dec 2026

MRT: Buona Vista (CC22 / EW21), Exit A or C, then a sheltered walkway, about 5 min on foot.

Bus: along North Buona Vista Road — 74, 91, 92, 95, 191, 196, 198, 200. Along Commonwealth Ave West — 105, 106, 147, 185, NR5, NR8.

Driving: enter via Vista Exchange Green or Rochester Drive; 800+ lots on-site, plus overflow at Rochester Mall, The Metropolis, Biopolis and Fusionopolis.
